Cyberparadism belongs to no one. It took shape in online aesthetics communities, was formalized on the Aesthetics Wiki and in the 2023 manifesto, and everything on this site is released under CC0 so it can be reused, remixed and built upon.

Artwork and writing that fit the aesthetic are welcome. The [contribute page](@/contribute.md) explains what fits, how provenance is stated, and how to submit.
