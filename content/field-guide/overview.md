Cyberparadism is a **techno-optimist futuristic aesthetic** that juxtaposes **lush or dramatic natural environments** with a **small, highly advanced human habitat**: typically a "breakaway" settlement that uses sophisticated, often plausibly speculated technology to achieve comfort, resilience and a sense of paradise. It emerged in online aesthetics communities in the 2010s, with earlier precursors sometimes cited, and it is discussed primarily as a visual and narrative style rather than a formal political program.

Cyberparadism is frequently described as *post-cyberpunk* in mood. It keeps the "high tech" but does not center the "low life" social decay associated with cyberpunk. Its defining image is not the endless neon city but **an enclave**, minimal, intentional and technologically mature, **embedded within** wilderness, coasts, mountains or terraformed frontiers.

## Terminology and scope

**Other names.** The term is sometimes paired with or replaced by **Cyber Paradise**, **Cybergenesis** and **Neogenesis** or **Neogenesism**. Usage varies by community.

**Aesthetic-first framing.** Cyberparadism is best understood as a *style system*: a cluster of recurring visual motifs, compositional habits and story tropes. When it is extended into real-world inspiration, for architecture, product design or lifestyle moodboards, it remains primarily aesthetic: a set of cues for what the cyberparadise looks and feels like, rather than a blueprint for governance. The [manifesto](@/manifesto.md) says the same in its own words: it is not a political or economic manifesto.

## Core ethos

Four commitments run through nearly every work that reads as Cyberparadist:

- **Optimism about technological maturity.** Advanced tools are depicted as enabling abundance, safety and leisure rather than surveillance, scarcity or collapse.
- **Nature as the primary stage.** The surrounding environment is not an afterthought. It is the dominant canvas.
- **Enclosure and community.** Humans gather in designed centers, such as plazas, agoras, forums and open-air auditoriums, and the built world acts as a small, coherent island inside a larger landscape.
- **Minimalism by sophistication.** Technology is depicted as *cleaner* and less materially noisy: fewer visible machines, fewer cluttered interfaces, more seamless integration.

A motto commonly quoted in community descriptions compresses all four:

> "Sophistication of technology is the gateway to abundance and prosperity."
